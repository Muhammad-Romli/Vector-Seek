# All About Dogs

Dogs (*Canis lupus familiaris*) are domesticated carnivores known for their loyalty, companionship, and intelligence. Often referred to as "man's best friend," dogs have lived alongside humans for tens of thousands of years.

### Key Characteristics
- **Social Nature:** Highly pack-oriented, forming strong emotional bonds with human families.
- **Senses:** Exceptional sense of smell and acute hearing, making them great working animals (search and rescue, service dogs, hunting).
- **Varied Breeds:** Over 300 distinct breeds ranging in size from tiny Chihuahuas to giant Mastiffs.
- **Lifespan:** Typically 10 to 14 years depending on size, health, and breed.


# All About Cats

Cats (*Felis catus*) are small, carnivorous mammals prized for their independence, agility, and companionship. Domesticated thousands of years ago, cats remain one of the most popular household pets globally.

### Key Characteristics
- **Behavior:** Independent yet affectionate, known for purring, grooming, and hunting instincts.
- **Agility:** Flexible bodies, quick reflexes, and keen night vision adapted for stalking prey.
- **Communication:** Communicate through meowing, body language, and scent marking.
- **Lifespan:** Indoor cats typically live 12 to 15 years, with many reaching their late teens or early twenties.



# All About Kittens

A kitten is a juvenile cat that relies heavily on its mother or caregiver during early development. Known for their high energy, curiosity, and playful nature, kittens go through rapid growth in their first few months of life.

### Key Development Stages
- **0–2 Weeks:** Born blind and deaf; entirely dependent on mother's milk and warmth.
- **2–4 Weeks:** Eyes open, hearing develops, and they start learning to waddle and explore.
- **4–8 Weeks:** Weaning begins, socialization speeds up, and pouncing/play instincts emerge.
- **2–6 Months:** High-energy play phase, rapid physical growth, and establishment of personality.
